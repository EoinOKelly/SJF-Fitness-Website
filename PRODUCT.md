# PRODUCT.md · SJF Fitness Portlaoise

## What it is
A private, one-to-one personal training studio run by **Sandra Furney** in Portlaoise, Ireland. Sandra is a certified Personal Trainer, Nutrition Expert and Trained Chef. Training happens in a private premises (The Office Box, Kealew Business Park), not a public gym.

## Unique mechanism
Bespoke 1:1 coaching from a single expert who owns the whole chain: training, nutrition, and real cooking knowledge as a trained chef, delivered privately, never on a crowded gym floor.
